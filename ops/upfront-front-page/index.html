Questions

Common questions

What does it cost?

Our prices are on this page. The discovery call is free, the Lead Response Audit is $99, the Operations Checkup is $750, projects start at $3,500 with the price fixed in writing first, and the Fractional Operations Partner starts at $2,000 a month.

How long does it take?

The Lead Response Audit scorecard arrives within 48 hours. The Operations Checkup takes about a week. Projects get a written scope and timeline before we start.

Do we have to switch software?

No. We don't sell software. We recommend what fits your business and budget, and we work with the tools you already own whenever we can.

Is there a long contract?

No. One-time services have a fixed scope and price. Monthly plans cancel with 30 days' notice. One-time services get a full refund if you cancel before work starts; after that, refunds are prorated to unfinished work. Details are in our Service Terms.

Is this right for a small team?

Yes. Small teams often feel these gaps the most, because the owner ends up being the connection between every department.

Do you work with businesses anywhere in the U.S.?

Yes. We work in person or by video, whatever fits you. We can visit anywhere in the U.S.; for visits outside our home state, you cover our travel costs, agreed in writing before we book.

Can you guarantee more sales?

No. We give honest recommendations and do the work described for your service, but we can't guarantee specific sales, leads or revenue.

What happens after a project ends?

Everything we build is written down, stays in your accounts, and your team is trained on it. You can run it yourselves or keep us on for monthly support.
