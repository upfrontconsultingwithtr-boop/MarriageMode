30-Day Boundaries Plan

Small steps, one week at a time. Do them together where it says so, and on your own everywhere else.

Week 1 · Close it

☐ Finish your audit at Marriage Mode (every app, every step)

☐ Cancel every subscription where you paid

☐ Search every inbox you use for old dating emails

Week 2 · Talk

☐ Fill in the Digital Boundaries worksheet together

☐ Agree what's okay for follows, DMs and old friends

☐ Write down one thing you each appreciated this week

Week 3 · Tidy

☐ Review contacts and old chat threads (your choice what stays)

☐ Delete saved dating logins from your password manager

☐ Check card statements for anything you missed

Week 4 · Check in

☐ Each re-run the email search on your own

☐ Talk: what felt good about doing this?

☐ Set your next check-in date
