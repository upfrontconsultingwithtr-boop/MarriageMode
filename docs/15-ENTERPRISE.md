# 15 · Enterprise Expansion Plan

## Who the enterprise buyers are, and who they aren't

**Yes:** organizations that already help couples prepare for and strengthen
commitment, and that will use the product *with* the couple:

| Segment | Why they buy | Entry offer |
|---|---|---|
| Premarital counselors & therapists | Structured homework on digital boundaries; progress the couple opts to share | Practitioner $29/mo |
| Churches & faith communities (many require premarital programs) | A modern module for existing classes | Organization from $199/mo |
| Relationship coaches & coaching companies | Differentiated program content | Practitioner → Organization |
| Wedding planners & venues | Client gift, referral revenue | Free referral partner |
| Employee assistance programs (EAPs) | Benefit for employees' relationships, delivered privately | Custom, year 2 |

**Never:** employers monitoring staff, or any buyer who would see an
individual's data without that person's opt-in. Enterprise admins see counts
and opted-in progress only.

## Product additions by stage

| Stage | Adds |
|---|---|
| E1 (M6) | Practitioner accounts, branded invite links, premarital "Digital Boundaries" module (worksheet + guided session) |
| E2 (M10) | Organization admin, seats, bulk invites, usage reports (aggregate), invoice billing |
| E3 (year 2) | SSO (SAML/OIDC), data processing agreement, SOC 2 readiness (paid, founder decision), API for counseling platforms |
| E4 (year 2+) | White-label for large networks; Spanish and other languages |

## Sales motion

1. 10 counselor conversations in M1 (free), offering the worksheet.
2. 3 pilots (free for 60 days) → convert at $29/mo.
3. Case study from **real** pilot results only.
4. Denominational and counseling associations: one partnership a quarter.
5. Conferences only after paid pilots exist (travel is a spend decision).

## Compliance path

- Not a medical provider: avoid storing therapy notes; practitioners keep
  clinical records in their own systems. This keeps HIPAA out of scope unless
  we sign agreements later (legal decision).
- DPA template and subprocessors list (Supabase, Netlify, Stripe, AI providers) ready by E2.
