The problem

Commitment happens offline. Your digital life doesn’t follow.

Profiles stay live

Deleting the app doesn’t delete the account. Old profiles keep showing up to strangers.

Billing keeps running

Subscriptions renew quietly through Apple, Google or the website.

Emails keep arriving

“You have 3 new matches” lands in an inbox a partner can see.

Each one is a small, avoidable trust risk.
