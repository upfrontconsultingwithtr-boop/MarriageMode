# 09 · Marketing and Launch Strategy

## Positioning

**For** people who just committed to one person, **Marriage Mode is** the
private checklist that closes your dating past completely, **unlike** spy
apps or "trust me" promises, **because** you do it yourself and choose what
to share.

Tagline options: "Close your dating chapter. Completely." · "Commit online, too." · "Nothing old in the way."

**Banned words:** catch, spy, track, cheater, monitor (see `TRUST-AND-SAFETY.md`).

## Channels (free first, ranked by expected return)

| # | Channel | Play | Weekly effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **SEO** | One guide per platform: "How to delete your [App] account for good (and stop the billing)". 29 platforms = 29 pages with steady search demand, each ending in the free audit. | 3 pages/week |
| 2 | **Short video** (TikTok, Reels, Shorts) | "POV: you got engaged and Hinge is still emailing you"; 20-minute cleanup timelapse; couple reacting to share card. | 5 posts |
| 3 | **Counselors & clergy** | Free "Digital boundaries" worksheet for premarital programs; 10 conversations in M1 | 10 emails |
| 4 | **Reddit** (r/relationship_advice, r/weddingplanning, r/dating) | Answer real questions helpfully; link only where rules allow | 3 answers |
| 5 | **Wedding vendors** | Planners and photographers share it as a client gift; referral link | 5 vendors |
| 6 | **PR** | Story: "The engagement to-do list nobody tells you about"; Valentine's and wedding-season hooks | Monthly pitch |

Paid ads: none until free channels show conversion (spend is the founder's call).

## Launch plan

| Phase | When | Actions | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Soft launch | Week 1 (now) | MVP live; share with friends, family and the LLC's social accounts; first 5 SEO pages | 25 audits |
| Test | Weeks 1–4 | SEO + video + 10 counselor conversations | 50 waitlist / 200 audits |
| Paid launch | Jan 2027 | Cleanup Pass on; email the waitlist; Product Hunt | 20 paid |
| Valentine's | Feb 1–14 | "Clean slate for Valentine's" campaign; couple challenge | 2× weekly audits |
| Couple Mode | Mar–Apr | Waitlist first; founding-member price | 100 couples |
| Wedding season | May–Jun | Practitioner plan, vendor referrals | 3 practitioners |

## Referral program

Couple plan: "Give a friend a free Cleanup Pass, get a month free" (both
sides credited after the friend completes an audit; no cash payouts).

## Content calendar, month 1

Week 1: Tinder, Hinge, Bumble guides + 5 videos · Week 2: Match, POF,
Facebook Dating + counselor worksheet · Week 3: OkCupid, eharmony, Grindr +
Reddit answers · Week 4: "Engagement digital checklist" pillar page + results recap.
