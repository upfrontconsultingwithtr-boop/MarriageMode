# 14 · Monetization Plan

## What people pay for

Cleanup is a one-time job, and trust is ongoing. Charge once for the job
and monthly only for things that keep running: alerts, the coach, Couple
Mode. Charging a monthly fee for a one-time checklist would cause high
churn and refund requests.

## Plans (recommended launch prices)

| Plan | Price | What's in it | Why it exists |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 forever | Full self-audit, catalog, email search link, share card, guides | Top of funnel; trust; SEO |
| **Starter: Cleanup Pass** | **$19 one time** | Synced account across devices, data-file scanner (Takeout/Facebook), a dated cleanup report (PDF), monthly re-check reminders for 12 months | Captures the one-time job |
| **Premium** | **$6.99/mo or $49/yr** | Starter, plus Watch mode (new dating-signup alerts, once Gmail access is approved), AI coach (fair-use cap), private reflections | For people who want ongoing support |
| **Couple** | **$9.99/mo or $79/yr for two** | Premium for both, plus the shared couple space, goals and opt-in sharing | Core paid plan; both people must consent |
| **Family** | Not at launch | — | No clear buyer yet. Reconsider if users ask for it (e.g. adult family members planning a wedding). Recorded so it isn't forgotten. |
| **Enterprise: Practitioner** | **$29/mo per counselor** (up to 25 active couples) | Branded invite links, a premarital "digital boundaries" module, progress only where the couple opts in | Counselors, pastors, coaches |
| **Enterprise: Organization** | **From $199/mo** (up to 10 practitioners) | Admin, SSO later, usage reports, bulk invites | Churches, counseling centers, coaching firms |

Founding members (waitlist): 40% off the first year of Couple ($47).

## Costs per sale

- Stripe: standard U.S. card fee per payment (check stripe.com/pricing).
  One-time and annual plans lose less to the fixed per-payment fee.
- Native app in-app purchases: Apple and Google keep a commission (15% under
  their small-business programs). Sell on the web first; native apps later.
- AI coach: capped per user per month so the cost stays under 10% of the plan price.

## Rules

- No ads, no data sales, ever. They contradict the product promise.
- Cancel in two taps (Stripe Customer Portal). Refund the Cleanup Pass within 14 days, no questions.
- Show prices including any tax note before checkout.
