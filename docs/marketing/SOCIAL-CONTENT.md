# Social content pack 1: 10 short videos + 10 posts

For TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook. Free to make on a phone.
Rules: warm, kind, no shaming; never "catch", "spy", "track", "cheater"; never
fake reviews or numbers. Every post points to one link:
**https://upfrontconsultingwithtr-boop.github.io/MarriageMode/**
Hashtags (pick 4–6): #engaged #newlyengaged #relationshipgoals #weddingplanning #couplegoals #datingapps #marriagetips #justengaged

## How to film (any of them)
Phone upright, natural light, 15–30 seconds. Text on screen in big letters.
Use a trending, calm audio. Show the website on your phone screen where it says "screen".

## 10 video scripts

**1. "The engagement to-do nobody tells you"**
On screen: "Just got engaged? Add this to the list 👇"
Show: wedding to-do list on paper → "Close old dating apps" written at the bottom.
Voice/text: "Deleting the app doesn't delete the profile. Or stop the billing. This free checklist does it all in 20 minutes."
End: screen showing the dashboard. "Free, private: Marriage Mode (link in bio)."

**2. "POV: you got engaged and Hinge is still emailing you"**
On screen: that line. Show an inbox (blur names) with a "someone likes you" style email.
Text: "Here's how to stop all of them in one go." Screen: the Find tab's Gmail search.

**3. "3 things deleting the app does NOT do"**
1. Delete your profile. 2. Cancel the subscription. 3. Stop the emails.
"Marriage Mode walks you through all three, for free."

**4. "Clean slate in 20 minutes" (timelapse)**
Timelapse of ticking the checklist on the phone. Score ring filling to 100.
Text at end: "Clean slate. 🤍" + link.

**5. "We did it together"**
Couple on a couch, each on their own phone. Text: "Each of us closed our own old accounts. Nobody checked anybody's phone."
"Then we shared our progress in Couple Mode." Screen: Couple Mode cards.

**6. "Your subscription is still billing"**
Text: "Deleted Tinder in 2023? Check this." Screen: Apple subscriptions page.
"Most people forget this step. Free checklist in bio."

**7. "Trust isn't checking someone's phone"**
Calm talking head: "Trust is choosing to share. That's why we built a tool that only works on your own accounts. You decide what your partner sees."

**8. "The digital boundaries talk"**
Show the printable worksheet. "Engaged couples: do this 30-minute worksheet together. Free to print."

**9. "Forgot which apps you used?"**
"Search your own inbox in one tap." Screen: Find tab. "We never see your email."

**10. "Counselors, this one's for you"**
"Premarital counselors and pastors: here's free homework for your couples." Show worksheet. "Link in bio."
