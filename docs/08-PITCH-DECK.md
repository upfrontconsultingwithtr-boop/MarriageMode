**1 · Title.** Marriage Mode: close your dating chapter, completely.
By Upfront Consulting with Tiff & Reco LLC.
*One line: a private, self-led cleanup for people entering committed relationships.*

**2 · Problem.** Commitment happens offline; your digital life doesn't follow.
Old profiles stay live, subscriptions keep billing, match emails keep
arriving. Each one is a small trust risk.
*Story: the "why is Hinge emailing you?" moment.*

**3 · Why now.** Dating apps are normal: Pew (2023) found 3 in 10 U.S.
adults have used one. Most couples now meet with a digital past, and no
product helps them close it.

**4 · Solution.** A 20-minute guided audit: pick every app you used, follow
exact steps to delete, cancel and unsubscribe, find forgotten accounts from
your own inbox, then share proof with your partner if you choose.

**5 · Product.** Screens: audit picker → checklist with scores → email check
→ share card. Live today at the MVP URL.

**6 · Trust is the moat.** Self-led only, no surveillance, opt-in sharing,
instant unlink. App-store safe, counselor-safe and press-safe. A competitor
that adds partner monitoring can't follow us into churches and counseling
practices.

**7 · Market.** About 2 million U.S. marriages a year (CDC, 2022), plus
engagements and new exclusive relationships. Professional channel: premarital
counselors, clergy, relationship coaches. *Show the bottom-up model from doc 14.*

**8 · Business model.** Free audit → $19 Cleanup Pass → $79/yr Couple →
$29/mo Practitioner → Organization plans. No ads, no data sales.

**9 · Go-to-market.** SEO ("how to delete [app]" searches), short-form video,
counselor and church partnerships, wedding vendors. All free to start.
