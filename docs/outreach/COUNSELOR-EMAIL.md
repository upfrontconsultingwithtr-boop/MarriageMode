relationship coaches. Send one at a time, personally, to people whose public

free tool called Marriage Mode.

One thing couples rarely talk about: old dating profiles that stay live,
subscriptions that keep billing, and "someone liked you" emails that keep
coming after they commit. We made two free things for that:

- A printable Digital Boundaries worksheet for couples:
  https://upfrontconsultingwithtr-boop.github.io/MarriageMode/worksheet/
- A private checklist to close old accounts (nothing leaves their phone):
  https://upfrontconsultingwithtr-boop.github.io/MarriageMode/audit/

It's built on consent: each person cleans up their own accounts and shares
only what they choose. No tracking, no checking each other's phones.

couples, and what you'd change? Your feedback would shape what we build next.
