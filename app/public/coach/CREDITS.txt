Mia's 3D avatar (mia.glb) is the "mpfb.glb" example avatar from the TalkingHead project
(https://github.com/met4citizen/TalkingHead), created with Blender and MPFB (MakeHuman),
licensed CC0 (public domain). Compressed with glTF-Transform.
TalkingHead (MIT) and HeadTTS (MIT) by Mika Suominen. Kokoro-82M voice model (Apache 2.0).

ARCHIVED (Nov 2026, files in archive/mia-v1/): Mia's earlier video face was cut from the free stock video
"Woman Talking In Front of Camera" (Pexels video 8048249, https://www.pexels.com/video/woman-talking-in-front-of-camera-8048249/),
used under the Pexels License (free for commercial use, modification allowed). The person shown is an actor
in stock footage; Mia is an AI and the actor does not endorse Marriage Mode.

Mia lip sync (mia-mouths.webp, mia-lips.json): mouth shapes cut from the same Pexels clips by Marriage Mode,
face positions measured with MediaPipe Face Landmarker (Apache 2.0). Built in-house, free.
